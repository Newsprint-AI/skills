# Interview record: product launch

Tarnby Freight Systems is a fictional company. Any resemblance to a real company is coincidence.

1. **Who:** Tarnby Freight Systems. Product: the Tarnby R4.
2. **What happened, and when:** Launching the Tarnby R4, a replacement gearbox for rail freight car doors, made at its Erie, Pennsylvania plant.
3. **Key facts (product launch):** Until now, rail operators in North America bought this part from one supplier in Germany, with a 9-to-14-week lead time. The R4 ships in 5 business days. Price is $2,850 per unit, about 10% less than the imported part. It fits the doors on the four most common North American covered hopper car models. (The user did not have the four car model names to hand.) Orders open Oct. 13, 2026, through tarnby.example/r4 or a Tarnby sales rep. The plant added 22 jobs to build it. Tarnby has repaired rail car components for 31 years. This is the first part it designs and builds itself instead of repairing someone else's.
4. **Why it matters:** A hopper car with a broken door can't be loaded, so it sits in the yard until the part arrives. Operators keep spare gearboxes on shelves to cover the long wait, which ties up cash. A five-day part means fewer idle cars and smaller spare inventories.

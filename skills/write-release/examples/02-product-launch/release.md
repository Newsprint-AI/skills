FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Tarnby Freight Systems Launches a Rail Car Door Gearbox Made in Erie That Ships in 5 Business Days

ERIE, Pa., Oct. 6, 2026 -- Tarnby Freight Systems is launching the Tarnby R4, a replacement gearbox for rail freight car doors that ships in 5 business days and is made at the company's plant in Erie, Pennsylvania.

According to Tarnby, rail operators in North America have bought this part from one supplier in Germany, with a lead time of 9 to 14 weeks. The R4 costs $2,850 per unit. Tarnby says that is about 10% less than the imported part, and that the R4 fits the doors on the four most common North American covered hopper car models.

Orders open Oct. 13, 2026, at tarnby.example/r4 or through a Tarnby sales rep.

A hopper car with a broken door cannot be loaded, so it sits in the yard until the part arrives. Operators keep spare gearboxes on shelves to cover the long wait, which ties up cash. A part that ships in 5 business days means fewer idle cars and smaller spare inventories.

Tarnby has repaired rail car components for 31 years. The R4 is the first part it designs and builds itself instead of repairing someone else's. The Erie plant added 22 jobs to build it.

"For years we rebuilt these gearboxes and watched our customers wait about three months for a new one," said Jane Ortiz, chief operating officer of Tarnby Freight Systems. "We decided we knew the part well enough to make it. Five business days instead of three months is the main point."
